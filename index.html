FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Mooshi free?

Yes. Most pets are free. Some premium characters can be unlocked by watching a short ad or with Premium, which also removes ads.

Why does it need "display over other apps"?

That Android permission is what lets a pet walk on top of other apps. Mooshi does not read what is on your screen.

Will it drain my battery?

Pets are lightweight sprites. There is a battery saver in Settings that pauses them when your phone is low.

Where do I get event codes?

In #event-codes on our Discord, during livestreams and at events. Open the app, tap Redeem on Home and type the code before it expires.

Can I make a pet from my own picture?

Yes. Tap Create on Home, pick your images and the editor turns them into a walking, climbing pet.
